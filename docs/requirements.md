# 1. Introduction

NovaCRM AI is a modern AI-powered Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
platform designed to help organizations manage customers, sales,
marketing, customer support, projects, communications, and analytics
through one centralized dashboard.

The application should support organizations of all sizes, from startups
to enterprise businesses.

---

# 2. Vision

Build one of the most modern CRM platforms with AI automation,
beautiful UI, enterprise scalability, and seamless workflows.
